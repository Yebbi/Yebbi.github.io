I am a Hedrick Assistant Adjunct Professor in the Department of Mathematics at UCLA, where I work with Stanley Osher.



My research focuses on developing new mathematical formulations to fundamentally improve computational approaches for challenging problems. I am particularly interested in uncovering underlying structures, establishing connections between seemingly disparate fields, and designing principled algorithms guided by these insights.



My work spans both theoretical and applied areas, including scientific machine learning, optimal transport, partial differential equations (PDEs), 3D vision, neural implicit representations, generative modeling, and classical numerical analysis.



I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Seoul National University, advised by Myungjoo Kang.
